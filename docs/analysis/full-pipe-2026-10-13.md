# Full-pipe plant, 12 October 2026

The 32×512 kernel is a usable plant. Open loop, the pulse rate tracks the SM count from 82 down to 4, and board power falls with it, from 211 W to 121 W. One closed loop at the default gains, with no load step, held Z at 1.497 on 54 or 55 SMs for the whole settle. Every one-second sample from 4 s to 25 s was inside the 0.08 band. Nothing was retuned.

The kernel is the test switch from the eighth pass: `GPUTRONIC_PEAK_ACC=32`, `GPUTRONIC_PEAK_ROUNDS=512`, `GPUTRONIC_THREADS=512`. The pulse batch and the control-word cache were on, as they were for the 47×10^12 FLOP/s hold. They stay off unless a run sets them. The unset kernel is unchanged. Kp is 0.55, Ki is 0.08, the deadband is 0.08, and the clamp is the scaled law, printed as 3.28 SMs of 82. The launch is 82 blocks. This was measured on the same RTX 5080, driver 595.99.02, CUDA 12.8, `sm_120`. Logs are in `logs/2026-10-13_full-pipe/`.

Issued FLOP/s is the measured pulse rate × 32 lanes × 32 chains × 512 rounds × 2. The SASS loop is one `FFMA` per chain per round. Modelled, not measured: 82 × 128 × 2 × 2850 MHz is 5.98×10^13 FLOP/s.

## Open loop

Each row is `noise` at that count for 8 s after calibration. The rate and Z are the mean of the last four telemetry lines. Z is against that run's own full-width reference, and it is clamped to 10, so the printed Z at 8 and 4 SMs is the ceiling. Power and clock are the flat part of `nvidia-smi dmon` after the calibration steps. No sample raised the power-violation or thermal-violation flag.

The rate is close to proportional to the count. At 4 SMs the rate is 2.26 million/s against 2.22 million/s from a straight fraction of the 82-SM rate. The clock steps up, from 2850 MHz at 82 SMs to 2895 MHz at 4, which covers that small extra. The true rate ratio at 4 SMs is about 20, and at 8 SMs about 10. The printed Z stops at 10.

Power follows the count on this plant. The light kernel sat near 85 W at every count, because the idle blocks spin and the work itself was cheap. Here 82 SMs draw 211 W and 4 SMs draw 121 W. The 121 W is the floor of this run: full clocks, four busy SMs, the display, and the idle blocks still spinning. Exit and relaunch were not turned on. About 90 W of the board power moves with the count.

The 82-SM row matches the eighth-pass quantum hold: 47.7×10^12 FLOP/s at 2850 MHz, against 47.0×10^12 at 2835 MHz that night. Same kernel, same fraction of the modelled peak, about 80%.

## Closed loop

`run 25` with the target at 1.5 and the gains left at the defaults. Calibration on this run: reference 46.0 million pulses/s, feedforward count 55.9, `sms_per_u` 31.2, integral clamp 1.313, printed as 3.28 SMs of 82.

From 4 s through 25 s, 22 one-second samples: mean Z 1.497, minimum 1.437, maximum 1.536, all 22 inside the 0.08 band. The count sat at 54 or 55, mean 54.4. The pulse rate averaged 30.7 million/s, which is 32.2×10^12 FLOP/s. After the calibration steps the board sat at 183 W and 58 °C, clock 2850–2880 MHz, violation flags clear. That is the same power as the open-loop row at 56 SMs.

The controller did not have to fight a load. It stepped onto the feedforward count and stayed there. The clamp was not the limit of this run. `work_scale_milli` was not used. On this kernel that scale skips pulses and leaves the FMAs running, so it is not a reduction in work.

## Left as they are

The switch stays off unless a run sets it. The gains stay at 0.55 and 0.08. The idle choice is still open: this sweep let idle blocks spin, and the floor at 4 SMs was 121 W. A disturbance that actually skips the FMA block has not been run.
