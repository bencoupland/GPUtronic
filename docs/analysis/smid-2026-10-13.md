# Physical SM id, and the holds at 36, 46, and 48

The three checks passed with the tally off and the idle-poll switch off.
Gold window mean Z was 1.423, transfer 1.463, episode 1.438. Open loop
with every block reporting: 36 SMs was flat (coefficient of variation
0.8%). 46 SMs still stepped between block 35 and block 36, by 1.85 times.
48 SMs stepped in that same place, by 1.74 times, so a count that fills
whole groups of 12 does not remove the step. Each block sat on its own
physical SM. One 46-SM hold with the idle blocks waiting 1 ms between
mapped reads kept the same step and shrank it to 1.27 times. Kp, Ki, the
deadband, and the clamp were not changed. GPU temperature stayed near 50 °C.

Runs on the RTX 5080, 12 October 2026, driver 595.99.02, CUDA 12.8, from
`controller-fixes` at `e768c27` plus the `%smid` slot in this branch.
Logs: `logs/2026-10-13_smid/`.

## What was added

The tally slot is now 40 bytes: `{ops_cuda, ops_tensor, t_ns, phys_sm, seq}`.
`phys_sm` is `%smid`, read once at block start. `seq` is still written last,
after the system fence. The slot stays keyed by `blockIdx.x`. An idle block
writes its SM id once, so the map includes blocks that never pulse.

`GPUTRONIC_IDLE_QUIET=1` makes an idle block wait on the GPU timer for 1 ms
between samples of the mapped control words. It is off unless that variable
is set. The checks were run with it unset. `sm_61` device-compiled. It was
not executed on a GTX 1080.

## The map

All four holds gave the same map. 84 physical SMs, one block each. No SM
carried two blocks. Block index is not the SM id. The launch walks in pairs
of consecutive SM ids, and the pairs are 14 apart:

| Blocks | Physical SMs |
|---|---|
| 0–11 | (0,1), (14,15), (28,29), (42,43), (56,57), (70,71) |
| 12–23 | (2,3), (16,17), (30,31), (44,45), (58,59), (72,73) |
| 24–35 | (4,5), (18,19), (32,33), (46,47), (60,61), (74,75) |
| 36–47 | (6,7), (20,21), (34,35), (48,49), (62,63), (76,77) |

Block 35 is physical SM 75. Block 36 is physical SM 6. The step in block
order is the boundary between those two waves of the launch.

## The three holds

Tensor sensor off. Tally on for every block. 1 ms host bins. 30 s open
loop. Means below drop the first second. At 100 ms the sum of the tallies
matched the pulse counter on every hold (ratio 1.000).

| Hold | Working blocks | Ops per host bin | CV | Largest neighbor step |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 36 | 0–35 | 401k–413k, mean 406k | 0.8% | none above 1.2× |
| 46 | 0–45 | slow 420k–433k, fast 787k–792k, mean 505k | 29.8% | 1.85× at block 35→36 |
| 48 | 0–47 | slow 423k–429k, fast 747k–752k, mean 507k | 27.7% | 1.74× at block 35→36 |

On the 48-SM hold the fast side is the whole fourth wave, blocks 36–47,
all twelve. They match each other. The first three waves match each other,
and they are the slow side. The partly-filled-group account predicted a
flat 48. These runs do not show that. The fourth wave is faster than the
first three even when all twelve of its SMs are working.

The 36-SM hold is only the first three waves. Those three are flat. That
part of the account stands. The step appears when the fourth wave is
turned on, and it stays when that wave is filled.

## Idle polling

The same 46-SM hold with `GPUTRONIC_IDLE_QUIET=1` used the same map. The
step was still between block 35 and block 36. The slow wave rose from
420k–433k to 699k–706k ops per host bin. The fast wave rose from
787k–792k to 893k–898k. The ratio fell from 1.85 to 1.27. The coefficient of
variation fell from 29.8% to 10.7%. The chip mean rose from 505k to 745k.

Idle blocks reading the mapped words on every trip are a large part of the
gap, and a large part of the lost rate at 46 SMs. They are not the whole
of the step. The switch stays off. Turning it on would change the plant
the checks measure.

## What stays put

Kp is 0.55. Ki is 0.08. The deadband is 0.08. The integral clamp stays the
scaled one. The tally stays out of the PI. The command stays one count for
the chip. The version string stays 1.0.0-gold.

The closed loop sits near 46 to 48 SMs. That is the edge where the fourth
wave turns on. That is a fact about this plant. It is not a new gain.
