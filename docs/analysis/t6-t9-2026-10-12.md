Kp 0.55, Ki 0.08, deadband 0.015, the values compiled into this commit. GPU temperature

## The new binary's free-run is lower

On this commit, tensor off, `rate_ref` during the four T6 calibrations was 435k, 439k,
449k, and 449k pulses/s. T9, which also turns on the device work counter, was 450k.

The binary from `test-results/2026-10-12_5080` (`42b88c9`), run on this same card just
after these tests, tensor off, same 64 threads per block, measured `rate_ref` 533255.
This morning that older binary was 555k–589k with the tensor sensor off. Part of the
gap is the hour. The rest is this commit. At scale 1.0 the new pass still reads
`work_scale_milli` from the mapped control block before it pulses. Z is a ratio, so the
loop can still track. The absolute rate is not the rate of the previous binary.

`n_ff` on the four T6 tables was 49.7, 50.6, 51.9, and 52.6. `sms_per_u` was 40–43.
The 63-SM table point was Z 1.19–1.26. The 42-SM point was Z 1.68–1.75.

## T6. A 1.2× pulse thin does not park on the clamp

`loadstep 60 1.2 20 40`. From 20 s to 40 s the kernel still does every pass and emits
one pulse per 1.2 passes. Mean of `z_hat` over the 10 ms bins:

| Window | Clamp 1.0 | Scaled |
|---|---|---|
| 15–20 s, before | Z 1.471, 49 SMs, I +0.83 | Z 1.470, 47 SMs, I +0.85 |
| 20–25 s, thin on | Z 1.598, 53 SMs, peak 1.739 | Z 1.593, 50 SMs, peak 1.720 |
| 35–40 s, still on | Z 1.555, 56 SMs, I −0.45 | Z 1.506, 50 SMs, I +0.24 |
| 55–60 s, thin off | Z 1.462, 50 SMs | Z 1.478, 47 SMs, I on the rail 37% of bins |

Neither run held the integral on the clamp during the thin. The count moved. The mean
came back to 1.55 and 1.51. It did not sit in 1.62–1.74. The analyzer still says FAIL:
its rule is that `z_hat` must stay inside ±0.02 of 1.5 for the rest of the window, and
the bins swing by about ±0.1 the whole time. That swing is already there before the step.

## T6. A 1.05× thin is inside the noise

`loadstep 45 1.05 15 30`.

| Window | Clamp 1.0 | Scaled |
|---|---|---|
| 10–15 s, before | Z 1.455, 48 SMs | Z 1.466, 47 SMs |
| 25–30 s, thin on | Z 1.500, 49 SMs, peak 1.606 | Z 1.500, 47 SMs, peak 1.618 |
| 40–45 s, thin off | Z 1.471, I at 1.0 for 93% of bins | Z 1.486, I at the rail for 56% of bins |

The mean was on 1.50 during the hold. The count moved by about one SM. After the thin
came off, the integral wound up to the clamp while the mean stayed near 1.47. The
deadband is 0.015 and the bins move by more than that, so the integral keeps winding
on ordinary samples.

## T7. Bursts

`bursts 1.2 1 5`, scaled clamp, tensor off. The log is one row per 10 ms, so a 2 ms or
4 ms burst often lands between rows. Those two lengths do not appear as their own
segments. What did land:

| Bin width | Count | Mean rise in Z | Mean peak Z | Mean SM move |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10 ms | 8 | +0.12 | 1.58 | 3.0 |
| 20 ms | 2 | +0.11 | 1.59 | 3.5 |
| 50 ms | 5 | +0.20 | 1.67 | 4.4 |
| 200 ms | 5 | +0.21 | 1.68 | 4.2 |

On the quiet rows, scale 1, the SM count changed between 76% of successive bins. An SM
change during a short burst is the same walk. It is not evidence the burst was seen.
None of the bursts then stayed inside ±0.02 of 1.5 for 100 ms within 200 ms. The row
noise is wider than that band. The integral was on the clamp for 62% of the quiet bins.

## What to do with this

The scaled clamp and the clamp of 1.0 did the same job here, because both stop near
3.4 SMs and the 1.2× thin was handled inside that, mostly by the proportional term.
There is no measurement in this set that asks for a larger clamp.

The ±0.02 recovery rule cannot pass on this plant while `z_hat` swings by a tenth.
The 0.015 deadband is why the count walks and why the integral lives near the rail.
Those are the reasons section A of `NEXT.md` still comes before another load suite:
tensor sensor off in the source, deadband 0.08, the 4.5 s free-run wait, and the
device sleeps removed. This run did not change any of those.
