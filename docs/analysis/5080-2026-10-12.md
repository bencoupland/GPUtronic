## T1. Tensor sensor, open-loop dyno

Both counters rise as SMs rise, and both fall as SMs fall. At 4 SMs the tensor counter
delivers about 82 pulses in an 8 ms window. The pass line was 50.

CUDA-core wall rate, tensor off, then on:

| SMs | Off, pulses/s | On, pulses/s | Loss |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 84 | 555k | 337k | 39% |
| 56 | 411k | 281k | 32% |
| 42 | 321k | 224k | 30% |
| 4 | 32k | 21k | 35% |

The model said the CUDA-core rate would drop by up to about a third, and that the
tensor rate would be about a third of the CUDA-core rate. The drop at full width is
39%. The tensor rate is half the CUDA-core rate at every width, ratio 0.499 to 0.500,
including the calibration points. On this kernel the two Z values are the same number.
The selector has nothing to choose. Which sensor it starts on flips when the two
`n_ff` values print as the same number. That did not move Z.

With the tensor warp off, the full-width rate is 555k–571k pulses/s. That matches the
old law's ceiling on this driver. The warp is the tax, not the new controller.

## T2. Gold closed loop

Tensor sensor on, `run 40 1.5`. Last 30 s:

| | Model | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| Mean Z | 1.49 | 1.487 |
| Z range | | 1.347–1.662, standard deviation 0.062 |
| Time above 1.6 | never longer than 100 ms | 982 ms in total, longest streak 32 ms |
| SM count | 46–47, occasional ±1 | mean 48.2, range 44–52, mode 49 |
| Gate | | dyno PASS, closed loop PASS (20 s z_mean 1.448) |

The step from 84 SMs to 42 shows up in the rate inside 16 ms. At the step the rate
was 348k. Eight milliseconds later it was 303k. Sixteen milliseconds later it was 283k.
It was still falling. The change is visible inside two 8 ms windows.

Tensor sensor off, last 20 s of a 30 s run: mean Z 1.471, count mean 53 (range 50–58).
That run's table put `n_ff` at 55.4 because the 63-SM sample came back at Z 1.32.
Other runs sampled that point at Z 1.18–1.20 and put `n_ff` at 47–50. A 300 ms sample
on the shoulder between 63 and 42 SMs moves the starting count by about 8 SMs. The PI
trims it back into the band. The count it settles on is not a stable property of the plant.

Request Z = 1.0, tensor off, after calibration: mean Z 1.002, mean count 83, range
79–84. Fifty-seven percent of the samples are at 84. The table sets `n_ff` to 84,
because Z 1.0 is the full-width end. When a sample dips under 1.0 by more than 0.015
the two-sided law turns a few SMs off. It lives on the ceiling. It does not lock there.

## T3. Transfer and episode

First pass, both dynos PASS, both closed loops FAIL the gate. The failure is the
free-run sample, not the tracking.

`mode_closedloop` slept 2.2 s and then measured a 1 s window as the free-run. The
table probe now runs until about 3 s. The window landed on a probe step. Transfer's
"free-run" came back at 3.73 GFLOP/s against a real full-width rate of about 6.3.
The closed loop was at 4.50 GFLOP/s and z_mean 1.494, so `useful_drop` was false.
Episode's "free-run" came back at 12.4 GFLOP/s against a real 18. The closed loop
was also about 12.5, z_mean 1.444, and the integral was on the clamp of 1.0 for part
of that run. Count about 20.

The wait in both checks is now 4.5 s, then full width, then the same 1 s window.
That is the only source change. Kp, Ki, the deadband, and the table were not touched.
Re-run:

| | Free-run | Closed loop | z_mean | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Transfer | 6.46 GFLOP/s | 4.42 GFLOP/s | 1.456 | PASS |
| Episode | 18.29 GFLOP/s | 12.46 GFLOP/s | 1.468 | PASS |

The suite's band is 1.40–1.55. Both are inside it. The model said Transfer 1.46 and
Episode 1.50. Transfer landed on the model's number. Episode is a few hundredths
under, with the count around 23 (the table asked for about 27). The first episode
run, the one that sat on the integral clamp, was 1.433. The clamp times Ki times
`sms_per_u` is only about 4 SMs of authority, so a low `n_ff` cannot be walked far.
That is why one episode run was short of the band's middle and the next was not.

Episode dyno R² was 0.82. Half the SMs (42) was Z 1.19. The line still bends. The
four-point table sees that: Z at 63 SMs is about 1.05, at 42 about 1.17, at 21 about
1.63. `n_ff` comes out near 27. That is the right part of the curve. The old half-SM
probe on this plant barely moved Z and asked for a much larger drop.

## T5. Reserve

Tensor sensor on, last 20 s. The model said Z about 1.48 at k = 0 and about 1.43 at
k = 4, with a little more throughput per reserve SM.

| Reserve | Mean Z | Mean SMs | Integral | Pulse rate |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 1.491 | 47.6 | 0.27 | 271k |
| 1 | 1.481 | 47.0 | 0.56 | 254k |
| 2 | 1.477 | 46.0 | 0.69 | 259k |
| 4 | 1.456 | 47.2 | 0.93, touching 1.0 | 264k |

Z falls as k rises. The extra SMs do not stay on. The integral spends itself removing
them, and at 4 it is against the clamp. The absolute rate did not rise. Each run's
own full-width reference also moved (about 375k–403k), so the rate column is not a
clean throughput comparison.

## What this does not say

- Kd stays 0. The derivative still jumps. One closed-loop sample read dZ/dt of −1.16
  per second while Z itself was 1.42. Turning Kd on would follow that spike.
- The 0.015 deadband is smaller than the Z movement, which is about 0.05–0.07.
  The count steps across several SMs on Gold (about 44–52) and on Episode (about
  19–28). Holding the count would mean a wider band. That was not changed.
- The 2 ms drain inside `gputronic_reset_counters` was not exercised as its own test.
- `follow` and `share` are not on this branch. They were not run.
- T4 was not run. There is no GTX 1080 on this machine. `__nanosleep` is still in the
  Gold kernel, so sm_61 still will not build.
